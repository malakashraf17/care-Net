Care Net

Healthcare Appointment & Doctor Management System

Care Net is a healthcare appointment app that connects patients with doctors. My part was designing and building the Flutter frontend: login and registration, doctor discovery, appointment booking and profile management.
Tech stack
FlutterDartMaterial DesignResponsive UI

Log In

A calm, clinical login screen with email and password fields, a “keep me logged in” option, a forgot-password link, and a quick path to create a practitioner account.

Home & Doctor Discovery

Patients are greeted by name, can search doctors or specialties, filter by speciality chips (Cardiology, Dentistry, Internal Medicine, Orthopedics) and browse recommended doctors with rating, clinic, years of experience and a one-tap Book Appointment button.

Join Care Net

One registration flow for both roles. Switch between Patient and Doctor, then fill in title, full name, age, phone number and a password with a show/hide toggle.

Book an Appointment

Pick a date from the month selector, choose an available time slot, review price and duration, select Cash or Wallet as the payment method and confirm the booking.

Edit Patient Profile

Patients keep their details up to date: photo, full name, age, email, home address and phone number, saved with a single button.
